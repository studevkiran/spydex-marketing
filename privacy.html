Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Spydex Marketing ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and handle information you provide when you contact us through our website www.spydexmarketing.com.

1. Information We Collect

When you fill out our contact or consultation form, we collect the following details you voluntarily provide:

  • Full name
  • Phone number / WhatsApp number
  • Email address
  • Business / brand name
  • Service(s) you are interested in
  • Monthly marketing budget range
  • A brief description of your business and goals

We do not collect payment information, passwords, or any government identification through this website.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information you submit through our contact form is used solely to:

  • Respond to your enquiry and discuss your business requirements
  • Provide you with a customised digital marketing consultation
  • Send you relevant information about our services (only if you consent)

Your form submission is sent directly to our team via WhatsApp. No data is stored on our servers or any third-party database. Once your message is delivered to our WhatsApp, it is subject to WhatsApp's own privacy policy (Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd.).

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal information with any third parties for marketing purposes. Your details are used only by the Spydex Marketing team to contact you regarding your enquiry.

4. Cookies

Our website does not use tracking cookies, analytics cookies, or any advertising cookies. We use no cookie banners because we collect no cookie-based data from visitors.

5. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to external platforms (Instagram, WhatsApp, Google). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those platforms. Please review their respective privacy policies before sharing your data.

Since we do not store your data on any server, there is no database record to delete or modify. If you wish to withdraw consent or have any concerns about the information you shared via WhatsApp, you may contact us directly:
